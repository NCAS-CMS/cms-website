### Introduction to the Monsoon service

Monsoon (previously referred to as MONSooN or Monsoon3) is the HPC hosted at the Met Office in Exeter and is a key piece of underpinning infrastructure for the strategic partnership between the Met Office and NERC, which aims to improve collaboration between scientists funded by the two organisations by facilitating:

* Sharing of modelling results
* Joint development of software
* Access to Met Office data

The Monsoon service originally started on 1st December 2009 and the current system is its third iteration.

The Monsoon service currently consists of:

* 130 nodes (125 compute nodes, 5 shared nodes)
* 256 GiB of memory per node.
* 2 CPUs per node (AMD Milan).
* Each CPU has 64 cores (128 cores per node).



Further information on Monsoon can be found in the [Monsoon3 User Guide](https://code.metoffice.gov.uk/doc/monsoon3/index.html)
